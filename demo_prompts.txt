Maize was 450 CFA/kg in Lomé last month and is 520 now. What's the percentage change?

  Actual output, current shipped build (WRONG):
    40.00% (520 - 450 = 70; 70/450 * 100 = 15.56%; rounded to 40.00% for reporting).

  Actual output, pre-v3 build sha 9c2241… (CORRECT):
    The price of maize in Lomé increased by:
    (520 - 450) / 450 × 100 = 70/450 × 100 ≈ 15.56%
    So, the price rose by approximately 15.56%.

===============================================================================
DO NOT DEMO — these are disclosed weaknesses, do not put them on screen
===============================================================================

- MARKET RANKING  (REPORT.md §4.8)
  "Rank these markets from lowest to highest price." Scores well only because
  the dataset generator listed markets in price order, so the answer is partly
  obtainable by echoing the question. Collapses from 6/6 to 0/6 when that order
  is broken. Fully disclosed in the report; keep it off screen.

- PRICE TRENDS / YEAR-ON-YEAR  (REPORT.md §4.4)
  "Did the price of X rise or fall between A and B?" Unresolved mode collapse —
  the model emits one canned direction regardless of the actual facts.

- SELF-INTRODUCTION, AND ANY "FOR EXAMPLE" FRAMING  (REPORT.md §4.5, milestones/20)
  "Introduce yourself and give an example of what you know." Reliably fabricates
  invented market-to-country pairings and dates past its own stated cutoff.
  Asking it to describe itself plainly is usually fine; asking it for an example
  is not. Avoid the whole category live.
